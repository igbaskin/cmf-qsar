## A cyclodextrin cavity as a pocket

The complementarity score on the largest set it has had: one host, one cavity and over a thousand guests with a measured constant. The host is a molecule file whose sugars are its residues; every guest is put in the cavity, centroid on the centre and longest axis along the channel, and refined there by the local rigid-body placement. `r` is the Pearson correlation with log K over the guests.
