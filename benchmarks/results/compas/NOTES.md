## Substitutions and deviations from the task text

- cif:C.3r dropped: no carbon of three rings in a cata-condensed set
- No separate package or YAML configuration: the study is this benchmark script with the library module cmfqsar/pbh.py, as every other study of the repository.
- The steric field is the program's (Tripos well depths, widths scaled by the van der Waals radius, hydrogens included), not a unit-weight heavy-atom field.
- The field scales (unit mean kernel diagonal) are taken over the whole set rather than the training part, so that one stored kernel per attenuation serves every split; the scale is a constant per field and the difference is a rescaling of the mixing coefficients.
- The attenuations are chosen on a random subset of 2,500 training molecules (5-fold CV); the ridge is then chosen by 5-fold CV on the whole training part.
- The aromaticity field E is the Clar-sextet share of every ring (placeholder); a NICS-weighted ring field is a follow-up that needs a DFT calculation per ring.
- Literature numbers for the gap on COMPAS-1 are not filled in (table row 'TBD').
- The Nystroem scaling experiment on COMPAS-1x (--stage scaling) uses the attenuations and the ridge of the main COMPAS-1D model (no search on the 34k set), one random 80/10/10 split, and the landmark path of cmfqsar.lowrank with the compiled kernel engine switched on for the process; the feature map of the training part is built once and the cross-validation reuses it, which CMFModel.fit_low_rank does not do.

## Decisions

- Frame: heavy-atom inertia tensor with atomic masses; x along the smallest moment, z along the largest; the signs of x and y by the moments sum x^3, sum y^3, sum x^2 y, sum x^3 y, sum x y^3 in that order (cmfqsar.pbh.resolve_signs); molecules with equal in-plane moments take x along the largest third (else fourth) moment.
- The kernel-based choice among the four frames (alignment by prediction confidence) is implemented (cmfqsar.pbh.choose_frame_by_kernel) and off by default; the rule leaves nothing to choose for a planar molecule without symmetry.
- Ring classes from the fused-neighbour graph of the six-membered carbon rings; checked against the 'lalas' annulation strings of the table (0 disagreements).
- Kernel ridge regression with the bias of Eq. 12 (2013); inside the cross-validation the same solution is obtained from the eigendecomposition of the centred kernel (checked against krr_fit at every run).
- Attenuation grid (0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 5.0) A^-2 in the program's convention (exponent alpha/2); ridge grid (0.0001, 0.0003, 0.001, 0.003, 0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1.0, 3.0).
- GFN2-xTB charges with xtb * xtb version 6.7.1pre (5071a88) compiled by 'Marcel@Raven' on 2024-07-23; the input geometry is kept (no optimisation), no Fukui run.

## Open questions

- Whether the gap of the non-planar molecules is served by the planar frame.  Most of the ten-ring molecules hold a helicene-like fjord and are twisted: 89 % of the COMPAS-1D molecules have a heavy atom more than 0.5 A from the best plane (median of the largest distance 1.48 A, 95th percentile 2.91 A).  The frame is defined for them, the two faces differ, and a mirror image lands in the mirrored frame, so the model is not symmetric under reflection although the gap is.
- The size-extrapolation split trains on few molecules (the sets are dominated by ten-ring molecules), so its numbers mix extrapolation with a small training set.
- Published models on the same molecules (table.md, last table): the geometric graph networks of Weiss et al. 2023 reach a test MAE of 0.011 to 0.022 eV on a random 81/9/10 split (their code: gitlab.com/porannegroup/PBHs-interpretability), the sequence model on augmented annulation strings of Fite et al. 2023 0.05 eV (gitlab.com/porannegroup/lalas), and the descriptor forests of Zheng et al. 2023 R2 0.91. The field models here (MAE 0.11 to 0.13 eV) are an order of magnitude behind the graph networks on interpolation among isomers. The gap is set by the ring arrangement to a precision the overlap of wide Gaussians does not resolve: the chosen widths are the widest of the grid and the ridge the smallest, so the model asks for more resolution than it was given. Nothing published measures the size-extrapolation split, where the ring fields are positive and the descriptor forest is not.
- The resolution stage (table.md) answers where the resolution is: not in the width. The attenuation grid extended to 0.02-20 A^-2 and the ridge down to 1e-6 leave the linear {A,B,C,D,E} model at 0.11 eV MAE; the Gaussian kernel layer on the same kernel halves it to 0.056 +- 0.004 eV (R2 0.964 +- 0.005, three seeds), level with the published annulation-string network and ahead of the descriptor forests, three to five times behind the graph networks. The layered model is no longer linear in the field: the co-field and rule 3 belong to the linear model, and Shapley values are the reading of the layered one. Its width came out at gamma = 0.25-0.5 of the median squared distance, the widest of the grid, and a wider grid of gammas is one more run. On the size-extrapolation splits the layer gains as much: the five-group model trained on the molecules of up to nine rings predicts the 6,408 ten-ring molecules with R2 0.92 and RMSE 0.113 eV (linear: 0.70 and 0.213), where the descriptor forest is negative.
- The chosen hyperparameters sit at the edges of the grids: the steric attenuation at the top (its width is scaled by the van der Waals radius, so the same grid value is a wider Gaussian) and the ridge at the bottom (1e-4) for the sets with the ring fields. A wider steric grid and a smaller ridge are worth one more run; the float32 kernels limit the ridge to about 1e-5.
- Seed 0 of the random splits puts benzene, the only one-ring molecule, in the test part; the main model extrapolates it to 10-13 eV (computed 6.72), which is the whole difference between that seed and the other four (table.md gives the RMSE over the molecules of three rings and more).
- The Clar-sextet ring field (E), meant as a placeholder, is the single largest gain on top of {A,B,C,D}; the NICS-weighted field should be compared against it, not against the set without it.
- On COMPAS-1x the landmark approximation converges at 500 landmark molecules: 1,000 landmarks give the same q2 and test R2 to three decimals and the rank of the feature map grows only from 2,244 to 2,570 (9 fields x 1,000 landmarks would allow 9,000). The fields of these planar molecules in one frame span a few thousand directions, which is also why the exact 8,678 x 8,678 model of COMPAS-1D is not needed for them.
- The per-ring contributions (rule 3) are large and cancel: a ring on the +x side of the frame counts positive and one on the -x side negative, whatever its class, because the co-field has a smooth component across the frame and a ridge of 1e-4 lets the coefficients grow. Their sum is the prediction; the individual values read position as much as chemistry. A decomposition against a reference (the Shapley values of cmfqsar/shapley.py) is the next thing to try for this series.

## The later sets and the follow-ups of the plan (30 October 2026)

- HOMA ring field (group F): the harmonic-oscillator model of aromaticity of every ring from the bond lengths of the DFT geometry (Krygowski 1993 parameters for C-C, C-N, C-O, C-S, N-N, N-O; a ring with a bond to boron has no parameters and weight 0).  It stands in for the NICS field of the task text, which would need about a week of serial ORCA on this machine; --stage nics computes NICS(1) and NICS(1)zz (B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP, GIAO, ghost atom 1 A above each centroid) for the 179 molecules of up to seven rings and reports the ring-by-ring correlation with HOMA and with the Clar share (table.md).
- Delta-learning (target DELTA_eV): the DFT gap minus a straight line in the GFN2-xTB gap of the same molecule (COMPAS-1x lists every COMPAS-1D molecule), the line fitted on the training part of every split; the field model learns the residual.  The 'corrected' columns of COMPAS-1x are not used: they are fitted to another functional and are 2.2 eV from the B3LYP gaps.  The multi-task form (cmfqsar/multitask.py) was not run: the residual form is the standard one and the xTB value exists for every molecule.
- COMPAS-3D (peri-condensed): the same reader (xyz archive) and frame; a fifth annulation class 'peri' (a ring two of whose shared bonds touch, or fused to four or more rings); the C.3r indicator field is present; Clar sextets as before (the enumeration is over all independent ring sets, fine for up to ten rings).  Three seeds and the three size splits; sets D, ABCD, ABCDE, ABCDEF, each with the Gaussian layer as a third configuration.
- COMPAS-2D (hetero atoms): read from the gzipped SDF (neutral rows), a random subset of 10,000 of the 52,000 molecules (seed 0) so that the stored-kernel protocol applies; every ring of four to six atoms is a ring; the atom types add N, N.H, O, S, B, B.H; group R is one ring-centred field per ring type of the table (benzene, pyridine, pyrazine, borinine, 1,4-diborinine, 1,2-dihydro-1,2-diborinine, pyrrole, furan, thiophene, borole, cyclobutadiene); no Clar field (undefined for hetero rings).  Sets C, D, R, ABCD, ABCDR, ABCDFR, three seeds and the size splits.
- Non-planarity: the frame is defined for every molecule, and the largest distance of a heavy atom from the molecular plane is recorded in prepared_<set>.json (COMPAS-1D: median 1.48 A, 95th percentile 2.91 A, maximum 4.15 A; COMPAS-3D: median 1.21 A, 95th percentile 2.41 A, maximum 3.36 A; COMPAS-2D: median 0.13 A, 95th percentile 1.84 A, maximum 4.10 A).  The peri-condensed molecules with a cove or fjord region are twisted, and the two faces of their frame differ; the frame rule keeps the sign convention of the planar case and does not choose between the faces.
- Open: NICS on the full set (a week of this machine, or a cluster); the multi-task form of Delta-learning; HOMA parameters for boron; a frame from the skeleton rather than from the masses for the hetero-atom set, and then the 52,000 molecules of COMPAS-2 beyond the subset (the landmark path of --stage scaling applies to them unchanged); a kernel averaged over the two faces of the frame, so that a mirror image gets the same prediction.
