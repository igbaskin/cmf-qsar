## What an invariant model says about a guest

The rotation-invariant kernel has no co-field, but every one of its channels is a sum over atom pairs, so a prediction splits over the atoms exactly. The question is whether that split names the group that enters the cyclodextrin cavity. The numbers below are per heavy atom (hydrogens reported on their neighbour), from models fitted without the guest being explained, and the difference is the mean share inside the group minus the mean share outside it.
