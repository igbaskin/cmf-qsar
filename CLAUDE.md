# Writing style

All texts in this project (the manual, README, docstrings, benchmark notes, commit messages,
log and error messages, comments) will be read by non-native English speakers. Write plain
English:

- Use literal wording instead of idioms and metaphors. Say "gains", "adds" or "improves" rather
  than "buys"; "overstates" rather than "flatters"; "fair" or "reliable" rather than "honest";
  "no better than chance" rather than "a coin flip".
- Avoid jargon that is not standard in chemistry, QSAR or statistics; when a technical term is
  needed, use the standard one and define it at first use.
- Prefer short sentences with one idea each.
- Headings, table column names and option descriptions must be understandable on their own.
